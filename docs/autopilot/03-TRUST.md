# 03 · TRUST
*Funnel stage 3 — become the go-to. Consistent, high-value content builds the trust that makes an audience act on what you say. The laws get you watched (`01-STOP`/`02-HOLD`); this stage gets the viewer to ACT — the DM, the signup, the sale. Per-brand SPCL lean lives in `BRANDS.md`.*

---

## STAY CATEGORIZABLE — the pillar discipline (Laws 7 + 8)

The algorithm is a sorting robot. It can't push you until it knows your bucket, and it can't build trust with the right audience if your topics wander.

- **Hammer 3–4 fixed pillars until you're bored.** The audience seeing them is always new. Pillars are *what* you talk about; formats are *how* (the pillar × format grid is your idea machine — see `BRANDS.md`).
- **Balance across the pillars — don't let one hijack the brand.** Spread your posts across all 3–4 pillars (aim for one of each per batch) so you're known as the expert on the *whole* niche, not one corner of it. The trap: a single pillar or format goes viral, you over-double-down, and the brand collapses into that one thing — the high-ticket fitness coach who went viral on recipes and became "the recipe guy" with a $20 cookbook instead of his real offer. **Reconciles with Law 4 ("make ten more of the winner"):** that's a *format* win, not a pillar win. Ride the winning **costume format** across all four NoCrapDiet pillars (swaps · label-lies · myth-busting · cheap-healthy) — rotate the pillar underneath it. Otherwise you become "the bread-costume guy" and never build the health authority the 30-Day Challenge needs to sell. **Ride the format; rotate the pillar.**
- **IG embeds your whole account into one topic.** Staying in-pillar isn't optional there — it's the only way IG can categorize you at all. **TikTok categorizes per video**, so that's where you test off-pillar without poisoning the account.
- **Content is the targeting (Law 8).** The algorithm shows your video to people interested in its topic — so make content for your *buyer*. 2k views from real small-biz owners beats 50k from randoms. A smaller right audience is the asset; whatever you monetize later launches *to* them.

Consistency in your pillars upgrades a random test audience into a qualified one over time. That qualified audience *is* the trust.

## SPCL — the influence layer
*Rides on top of the laws. Influence = the likelihood someone complies with your ask. Stack all four into every video.*

- **S — Status:** show you control something people want. Proof you have the result (a ranked site, a real client, the truth they're missing). *Show the thing.*
- **P — Power:** give one tip the viewer can use *today* that actually works. A small win → they trust your next ask. **The strongest of the four.**
- **C — Credibility:** third-party proof that's hard to fake — before/afters, screenshots, real numbers, named results. Observable, not claimed.
- **L — Likeness:** be you. Your real voice and values. A sharp 17-year-old builder *is* the edge — don't sand it off. *(This is the Dopamine ladder's rungs 5–6 by another name.)*

### Proof is the AI moat (the risk continuum)
The higher the stakes for the viewer, the more demonstrable proof they need before they'll act. AI fakes a talking-head tip easily; it *can't* fake you holding the product and reading the real label, or walking a client's real site live. **Prioritize the formats AI can't replicate** — real-time demonstration to a real customer. That's why NoCrapDiet's on-camera label-read is locked, and why recorded SemSeo teardowns beat any scripted talking-head.

### The Trust Ladder — not all proof persuades equally
*AI-moat is one axis (can it be faked); persuasion is the other (will they believe it applies to them). This ladder ranks proof by persuasion. Climb as high as you can.*

1. **Bullseye (top) — proof that mirrors the viewer.** A one-to-one example that looks/sounds/struggles exactly like them ("the bar *you* grab every morning thinking it's healthy"), or your own result if you're in their demo. People trust a version of themselves getting the outcome far more than a statistic. (Psych: self-identification.)
2. **Warm crowd (middle) — a relatable "a lot of people like you."** Generalized but still a proxy for them.
3. **Third-party (bottom) — a study, stat, or article.** Works, but makes them ask "were those people like me?"

The move for NoCrapDiet: the on-camera **real label-read is your Credibility floor** (it's the AI-moat, hard evidence) — then *climb the ladder* by framing it at the viewer: not "studies show added sugar is bad" but "this is the loaf in your cart right now, flip it over with me." Same fact, bullseye framing. Never manufacture a fake example to climb — if the bullseye proof isn't real, drop to the rung that is.

### Gossip-whisperer, not town-crier — the delivery tone
*How* you say the proof decides whether it reads as trust or as a sales pitch. "HUGE news, this changes everything, pay attention NOW" trips every sales alarm — in 2026 people dismiss that tone on reflex. Lower it: "okay, barely anyone knows this yet…" — a peek behind the curtain, a secret shared *with* them, not announced *at* them. This fits NoCrapDiet's been-lied-to voice perfectly ("here's what they're hoping you don't flip the box to read"). Conspiratorial, not promotional.

### Trust anchor — proof in line 2–3, not the end
Don't save your credibility for the payoff. Right after the hook — the second or third line — drop one proof beat that answers *why this is true*: a result, an experiment you ran, the label you're about to read, the hours you put in. It credentializes everything that follows before they have a chance to doubt it. NoCrapDiet's is built in: "I read the back of 50 of these" or "flip it over with me" right after the hook line. Ask of every script: why should they trust this — and is that answer in the first few seconds, not buried at the end?

## THE FUNNEL TRUTH — a reel doesn't close anything

**Shorts attract; a deeper touch converts.** Don't expect a Reel to close a sale — its job is to earn the next step: a DM, a Loom teardown, a waitlist signup, a sales convo. That deeper step is where SPCL pays out and where the funnel hands off to `04-CAPTURE.md` and `05-SELL.md`.

### The profile audition — the follow happens off the video, on the profile
A viewer rarely follows from one reel. They tap your **profile** and audition you in about half a second: follower count first (a bigger count = borrowed trust and an instant-follow reflex; a tiny count = a reflex pass, however unfair), then your **pinned / top video**, then a 2nd–4th video, *then* follow or leave. If they don't follow, they re-audition every 3–7 days when you resurface — each view banked nudges them closer. Two implications: **(1) pin your single biggest banger** so the audition's second step is your best work, not whatever's newest; **(2) the profile itself is part of the funnel** — the bio says who it's for + the free guide, and the link in bio goes to nocrapdiet.com's guide page (`04-CAPTURE.md`), not a bare homepage. Every "link in bio" CTA dies if the bio link isn't loaded.

## HOW TRUST COMPOUNDS INTO AUTHORITY
Hitting `01-STOP` + `02-HOLD` consistently, in a voice that's unmistakably yours (Likeness), climbs the top of the dopamine ladder: **Affection** (they like *you*) → **Revelation** (your face alone earns the watch). That's authority — and it's measured in follower-per-view and return-viewer rate, not raw views (`RUN.md` measure).
