# 02 · HOLD
*Funnel stage 2 — keep them watching. The middle between the hook (`01-STOP.md`) and the CTA. This is the part most amateurs skip, and it's where retention dies. Per-brand rung weighting lives in `BRANDS.md`.*

This is **Kallaway's Dopamine Ladder**. Rungs 1–4 are *per-video* — you engineer them into every reel. Rungs 5–6 are *per-creator* — the compound interest of hitting 1–4 over and over. Superfandom is earned, not posted.

---

## THE SIX RUNGS

1. **Stimulation (the stun gun)** — the raw scroll-stop. Shock, pattern interrupt, motion, a number that shouldn't be true. First 2 seconds. *(This is Law 1 / `01-STOP.md` by another name.)*
2. **Captivation** — the stun gun + an **open question** planted in their head. Not "here's a fact" — "here's a fact that opens a gap you now need closed." The curiosity loop.
3. **Anticipation** — keep them **guessing at the answer**. The loop stays open while you build. The longer they guess, the longer they watch. **Most creators reveal too fast — this is where retention dies.**
4. **Validation** — close the loop. Rule: **the answer must be non-obvious — but logical in hindsight.** They couldn't have predicted it, yet they instantly accept it once they hear it (a head fake, not a random twist — plant the clues like a magician so the reveal lands as *inevitable*, not confusing). If they could've guessed it, the payoff is dead; if it makes no sense, they feel cheated. The aha is prediction error that still adds up. **Don't show the mascot:** never deflate your own reveal by admitting it's old news ("of course, people have known this for years"). Commit to the frame — don't hedge, don't downplay. The second you call your angle common knowledge, the intrigue you built evaporates.
5. **Affection** — they stop liking the video and start liking **you, the messenger.** Built by hitting 1–4 consistently in a voice that's unmistakably yours.
6. **Revelation** — your name or face alone makes them stop, *before they know the topic.* Top of the ladder. A personal-brand asset, not a video tactic. (This is the engine behind authority/authenticity — see `03-TRUST.md`.)

---

## HOW TO ENGINEER EACH RUNG

| Rung | The move | In a script |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 Stimulation | Open mid-sentence on the most shocking claim + big on-screen text + visible motion | "This 'healthy' granola has more sugar than a Snickers —" |
| 2 Captivation | Attach an open question to the shock. Name a gap, don't fill it | "— and it's the brand you'd *never* suspect." |
| 3 Anticipation | Stall the reveal. Add stakes / a second beat that keeps them guessing | "Most people eat this every morning thinking it's the smart choice." |
| 4 Validation | Reveal + the **non-obvious why** — the part they couldn't guess | "It's not even the sugar. They stripped the fiber, so it hits your blood like straight candy." |
| 5 Affection | Same blunt, been-lied-to voice every time. Be a *person*, not a feed | Your reaction, your take — consistent across 50 reels |
| 6 Revelation | A recurring hook/identity people follow regardless of topic | The "no-BS food guy." The format becomes the draw |

**Rung 3 is the one you're probably missing.** Every amateur jumps 1 → 2 → 4 and reveals on beat two. The pros sit in anticipation. On a 25-second reel that's one extra line of stalling ("here's the part nobody tells you…", "and it gets worse…") — but it's the line that doubles watch time.

> **Maps onto long-form.** Rungs 2–4 = the open-loop → stall → non-obvious-payoff body structure the `LONGFORM.md` Script Factory runs ("open a loop before closing the last one"). Same retention spine, both lengths.

## STAKES & THE SPECIFIC QUESTION — the front of the loop
The rungs jump from shock (rung 1) to open question (rung 2), but skip *why the viewer should care enough to start predicting*. That's the **stakes**: a character they can root for (you, them, "the bar you grab every morning"), something at risk (a consequence if they're wrong), and urgency (a ticking clock). Stakes aren't life-or-death — just *personally relevant*: "the 'healthy' breakfast you've fed your kid all year" beats "this cereal has sugar."

Then make the **big question specific**, never a vague teaser. "You won't believe this" gives the brain nothing to predict; "this bar has more sugar than which candy?" loads a real guess the reveal can then break. (Dopamine is the *prediction* chemical — no specific prediction, no payoff. It's why rung 3's stall works at all.)

---

## LINE-LEVEL CRAFT — the words under the rungs
Rung 3 is the *structure* (stall the reveal); this is the sentence layer that makes the stall actually hold. Both free, both applied at SCRIPT time (`RUN.md` Voiceover Factory — checks 2 and 4 there enforce this):

- **"But / therefore," never "and then."** Beats joined by "and then" are a list — no tension, easy to swipe off. Join beats with **but** (conflict) or **therefore** (consequence) so each line pulls causally into the next. Hunt every "and then" in a script and rewrite it as a but or a therefore.
- **Jagged rhythm.** Look down the right edge of your script. If the line lengths are all similar, it reads flat and droning. Vary them — short. Short. Then one longer line that builds. Short again. A jagged edge reads with momentum; a straight edge loses them. Rhythm *is* retention.

This is the layer your two 18–19% losers were missing — the reveal structure was probably fine, but flat "and then" pacing let them swipe before it landed.

## COMPREHENSION — watched ≠ absorbed
The single highest-leverage factor, and the one most creators skip: people watch without *understanding*. A non-obvious payoff (rung 4) is worthless if the point never lands in their head. Engineer for absorption:

- **One idea per video.** A reel that teaches two swaps teaches none. Pick the single point; cut everything else.
- **Bite-sized beats.** Feed it in chunks, not one dense block — a chicken breast cut into pieces gets eaten; the whole thing on a spoon doesn't.
- **Smallest words that work.** "They strip the fiber, so it hits your blood like candy" beats "it reduces the satiating glycemic matrix." Shorter words, simpler sentences.
- **One concrete, relatable example** beats an abstract claim every time. (Pairs with the bullseye proof in `03-TRUST.md`.)

The test for NoCrapDiet: could a viewer repeat your point to a friend ten seconds after watching? If not, it didn't land — views don't matter if the message bounces off. (This is also what makes a video **shareable**: people share what they *understood* and want to pass on.)

---

## SOUND-OFF — most of them are watching muted
~80% of feed viewers watch with the sound off. If your reel only lands with audio, most people get nothing — and for you it's worse, because your value lives in a **voiceover a muted viewer never hears.** So the **text hook + the visuals have to carry the whole message alone.** The test: play the reel on mute — can you follow lie → label → swap without a word of audio? If not, the on-screen text and the product/label shots aren't doing enough. Non-negotiable for NoCrapDiet: the label number and the swap must be *readable on screen*, not just spoken.

## THE PER-REEL CHECKLIST
*Baked into the Voiceover Factory prompt (`RUN.md`) as non-skippable checks — a script that fails any of these gets rewritten, not shipped.*

- [ ] **Rung 1 — Visual:** Does the first frame show motion / the product / a mid-action shot?
- [ ] **Rung 1 — Text:** Is the big on-screen claim a scroll-stopper on its own?
- [ ] **Rung 1 — Audio:** Does my first spoken line (or sound) hit in the first 2 seconds — and is it *different words* from the text hook?
- [ ] **Rung 2:** Is there an open question they now *need* answered? (not just a stated fact)
- [ ] **Rung 3:** Do I stall the reveal at least one beat? (stakes / "it gets worse" / "the part nobody says")
- [ ] **Rung 4:** Is the payoff **non-obvious**? Could a viewer have guessed it? If yes — dig deeper for the real why.
- [ ] **Rung 5:** Is this unmistakably *my* voice, the same person as my last 10 posts?
- [ ] **Mute test:** with the sound off, do the on-screen lines alone tell lie → label → swap?

Rungs 1–4 fail = the video dies. Rung 5 fails across the account = you stay forgettable even when individual videos pop.

---

## READING IT ON THE BOARD
The ladder is verifiable on your scoreboard: **rungs 1–4 show up in watch time / completion**; **rungs 5–6 show up in follower-per-view and return-viewer rate.** When a reel pops on views but bleeds follows, you nailed 1–4 and missed 5. When average view duration is low, you're revealing too fast — fix rung 3. Don't guess; read the board (`RUN.md` measure).

**Saves come from rung 4** (a non-obvious reveal worth saving). **Follows come from rung 5** (liking the messenger).
