# 04 · CAPTURE
*Funnel stage 4 — own the audience, don't rent it. Per-brand magnet specifics live in `BRANDS.md`.*

**A follow is rented; an email is owned.** The algorithm can bury your reach overnight — it can't touch your list. The free guide trades a small free win for an email, moving the right people off rented land (the feed) onto land you own (the list). The list is who the Weight Loss 30 Day Challenge launches to.

---

## THE MOVE — every reel feeds one path

```
reel → one CTA line → nocrapdiet.com guide page → email gate → free guide → list
```

1. **Every reel ends with ONE site-driving ask** (RULES.md CTA doctrine). Rotate the *mechanism* across a batch, never the destination:
   - *Comment SWAP* → reply with the guide link
   - *DM me SWAP* → reply with the guide link
   - *Free guide, link in bio* → bio link goes straight to the guide page
   - *It's free at nocrapdiet.com* → direct
2. **The guide page is the gate.** The guide ("27 Sneakiest Fake-Health Foods + the $3 swap for each") stays 100% free — but it's **delivered by email**: name-optional form, email in, PDF sent + shown. No email field = no list = the whole funnel leaks at the last step. This is the standard; if the site currently hands out the PDF ungated, gating it is the highest-priority site fix.
3. **Keyword replies:** until volume justifies automation, **Dennis replies manually** — a saved reply with the guide-page link, sent within 24h (comments answered publicly = social proof). **Trigger to automate:** the day SWAP comments/DMs pass ~10/day, set up IG keyword auto-DM (ManyChat free tier) firing the same link. Don't build the robot before there's traffic for it.
4. Email lands on the list → `emails` becomes the scoreboard (below).

"Follow me" rents; "comment SWAP, it's free" owns.

---

## INSTAGRAM STORIES — the reach button
Reels get you *discovered*; **Stories convert the followers you already have.** IG hands you a button to reach ~10% of followers on demand — TikTok and YouTube can't. Stories are a primary capture surface: most of the SWAP asks should live here, daily, low production.

**The 3-slide template** (keep it to 3 — every slide loses ~10% of viewers):
1. **Hook + proof slide** — a reel-grade line + a proof image (label screenshot, real number).
2. **Value slide** — three quick label tricks / swaps. A real micro-win.
3. **CTA slide** — "Reply SWAP and I'll send the free guide" → same link, same gate.

---

## CTA DISCIPLINE — don't trigger sales breath
Viewers sniff selling in 10 seconds and scroll. The ask disappears into a video that's good on its own:
- **Write the viral video first, add the CTA last.** The other 95% of the script stays untouched by the ask.
- **Keyword CTA on screen under 5 seconds.** In and out. Dwelling on the ask flips the viewer into "this is selling me" mode and *lowers* conversion.
- **Frame the ask as a give.** "Comment SWAP and I'll send you the list" is a gift; lean on *free* — zero friction. (Same instinct as the gossip-whisperer tone, `03-TRUST.md`.)

---

## THE VALIDATION GATE — don't skip
Ship the gated guide, measure **opt-in rate** (emails ÷ guide-page visits). If people won't trade an email for it *free*, they won't pay for the Challenge later. Fix the magnet before building the offer (`05-SELL.md`). Opt-in rate is the cheapest demand test you have.

## THE METRIC SHIFT
Once the gate is live, the scoreboard is **emails captured / week** — add an `emails` column to the tracker Google Sheet. Saves + follows demote to *leading* signals: they predict capture; capture compounds into revenue.
