## THE NOCRAPDIET LADDER — the only live one

```
free swap guide (email gate) → 5-email nurture → FREE Week 1 of the
Weight Loss 30 Day Challenge → paid Challenge (one-time)
```

**Free Week 1 is the conversion lever.** It's a low-risk yes that flips into the paid program — the first-decision effect. Content and nurture drive toward "try Week 1 free," never "buy my program."

**The nurture (build once, runs forever):** every captured email automatically gets —
1. **Day 0** — the guide + one line of voice ("flip the box before the cart").
2. **Day 2** — the single best label trick (pure value, no ask).
3. **Day 4** — a swap with the 12-month math (cost-of-inaction, `01-STOP.md`).
4. **Day 6** — the been-lied-to story: why NoCrapDiet exists (Likeness).
5. **Day 8** — the invite: "Week 1 of the 30 Day Challenge is free. See if it's for you."

Emails read like the reels: blunt, short lines, one idea each. Write the sequence once in the brand voice; it converts while Dennis is at school.

**Launch gates (in order — don't skip ahead):**
1. Email gate live on the guide page → watch **opt-in rate** (`04-CAPTURE.md`).
2. Nurture sequence live → watch **Week 1 starts / week**.
3. Week 1 → paid: watch **trial→paid rate**. If people finish free Week 1 and don't convert, the offer (not the traffic) is the problem — fix it before buying more reach.
